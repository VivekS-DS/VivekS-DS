### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Vivek S**, based in **Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu 🇮🇳**

I bring **10+ years of experience** in research, statistical analysis, and teaching at Anna University — and I'm now channelling that rigour into the world of **Data Science & Analytics**. My academic background in experimental design and multivariate statistics gives me a strong foundation that most data practitioners don't have.

I'm deeply passionate about **working with data** — finding patterns, asking the right questions, and turning raw numbers into insights that actually mean something.

- 🔬 Background in **Statistical Analysis, DOE, PCA & Response Surface Methodology**
- 🏗️ Built a full **ETL Data Lakehouse** — YouTube API → MongoDB → MySQL → Streamlit
- 📊 Designed Power BI dashboards analyzing **1.8M+ data points** across 5 years
- 🎓 **Top 5% nationally** — NPTEL Introduction to Machine Learning (2025)
- 🌱 Currently deepening skills in **Machine Learning, Forecasting & SciML**
- 💬 Ask me about **Python · SQL · Power BI · Statistics · Predictive Modelling**
- ⚡ I believe: *"Without data, you're just another person with an opinion."*

#### 🔷 YouTube Data Lakehouse & Analysis
> *Python · YouTube API · MongoDB · MySQL · Streamlit · ETL*

Built an end-to-end ETL pipeline that extracts YouTube channel data via API, loads it into **MongoDB as a Data Lake**, and transforms it to a **MySQL Data Warehouse** for structured EDA. Deployed an interactive **Streamlit** app for real-time visualization.

### 🙋 My Story

I spent over a decade at **Anna University** — teaching, researching, and solving complex engineering problems through data. As a Research Scholar, I regularly used **Principal Component Analysis, Design of Experiments, and Response Surface Methodology** to extract meaning from noisy, high-dimensional experimental data.

Now, I'm bringing that same analytical rigour into the world of **Data Science and Business Intelligence**. I'm not starting from scratch — I'm building on a foundation that most people in this field don't have: *real scientific discipline applied to real data problems.*

I'm passionate about working with data — not just running models, but asking the right questions, understanding the domain, and communicating findings in a way that drives action.

**I'm actively open to roles in:** Data Analytics · Business Intelligence · Data Science · ML Engineering
